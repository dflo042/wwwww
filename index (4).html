THE BORROWING SCANDAL

The Chalked File — League Inquiry No. 001 • Parties: 3 Headed Monster (Matt) & Daygos finest (Isaiah)
  • The Scheme

    Two franchises attempt to quietly "borrow" players — trade a player over for a week, then trade him right back. No harm, no foul. Allegedly.

  • The Snap Heard Round the League

    Mid-borrow, Tucker Kraft tears his ACL. The player can no longer simply be handed back like nothing happened. The scheme is exposed in the worst possible way.

  • The Controversy

    The league erupts. If borrowing is real, who owns the risk? The dispute becomes the founding season's defining constitutional crisis.

  • The Chalked League

    Out of the wreckage, new law: the Chalked League is established to make borrowing fair and bring the practice into the light.

  • The Settlement

    As restitution, Trevor Etienne — the player the two parties had borrowed between each other — is consigned to waivers, untouchable, for the remainder of the season. Justice, served cold, on the wire.

"Let the record show: in this league, even the loopholes have consequences."
